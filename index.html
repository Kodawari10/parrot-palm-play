How to play

  1. Tap the palm to pocket ripe seeds. A new one grows every 20 seconds, up to three on the tree. Finishing an island makes the next one grow a little faster.
  2. Pick a seed, then tap a parrot. They crack it and reveal a map tile.
  3. New tiles wait in the tray. Drag one onto its spot, or tap it and then tap the slot. Auto-place all is there if you want it. Sixteen spots finish an island.
  4. Duplicates visit the nest in the album and come back as more seeds. Two common spares make one plain seed.

A plain seed shared with Mango lands on these rarities. Pip favors epic pieces. Nova leans legendary, and finds the extra tile a little more often. Lucky and golden seeds (from the nest) shift the odds upward. Extra is the rarest tier, and Flower Nook is the rarest single piece.

Seeds open the island you have selected. If a streak brings nothing new, later seeds lean toward the missing common, epic, and legendary pieces. Finishing that invites Sol and opens Moonpearl Cay. Progress stays on this device. A Starknet wallet is optional.
